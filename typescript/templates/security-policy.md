## Report A Vulnerability

Send reports privately through <PRIVATE_REPORTING_CHANNEL>. Do not open a public issue for an unpatched vulnerability.

Include, when safe:

- affected version and component;
- reproduction steps or proof of concept;
- impact and prerequisites;
- suggested mitigation;
- preferred contact for coordinated follow-up.

We will acknowledge within <ACKNOWLEDGEMENT_TARGET>, provide a triage update within <TRIAGE_TARGET>, and coordinate disclosure after affected artifacts and mitigations are available. Do not include real credentials, personal data, or production payloads.
